Explore Manali

Popular places to add to your journey.

Solang Valley

Near Manali, Himachal Pradesh — known for its wide mountain views and seasonal adventure activities.

Rohtang Pass

About 50 km from Manali — a high mountain pass famous for dramatic Himalayan scenery. Access is seasonal and permit-dependent.

Hadimba Devi Temple

In Old Manali — a historic wooden temple set among tall cedar trees.
